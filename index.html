El Salvador

The country of the eternal smile

General Information

  • Capital: San Salvador
  • Official Language: Spanish
  • Area: 21,041 km²
  • Population: 6.3 million (approx.)
  • Currency: United States Dollar (USD)
  • Government: Presidential Republic

El Salvador is the smallest country in Central America. Known for its beautiful landscapes, rich culture, and warm people.

Important Milestones

  1. 1524

    The territory was colonized by the Spanish.

  2. 1821

    El Salvador declared its independence from the Spanish Empire on September 15, 1821.

  3. 1932

    The Massacre: a tragic event in the country's history that marked a social and political turning point.

  4. 1992

    The Chapultepec Peace Accords were signed, putting an end to the civil war.

  5. 2001

    El Salvador adopted the U.S. dollar as its official currency.

  6. Today

    A country that continues to grow, innovate and build a better future.
