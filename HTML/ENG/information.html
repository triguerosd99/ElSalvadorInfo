National Facts & Symbols

Izalco Volcano & Landscape

National Land

A stunning active volcano and natural symbol of our varied volcanic topography.

National Bird: Torogoz

Fauna Symbol

The turquoise-browed motmot, known for its racquet-shaped tail and beautiful plumage.

National Flower: Izote

Flora Symbol

The blooming Flor de Izote, representing the purity and resilience of the national character.

Education Investment

Modern Learning

Investing in curriculum reform, modern facilities, and digital literacy to empower future generations.
