Geography of El Salvador

A journey through the varied and beautiful landscapes of our land.

Geographic Features

Santa Ana Volcano

Highest Point

The highest active volcano in El Salvador, know for its beautiful turquoise crater lake.

Pacific Coast

Surf & Beaches

Known for its world-class surf breaks, rock formations like El Tunco, and vibrant coastal culture.

Lempa River Basin

Major Waterway

El Salvador's largest and most important river, crucial for agriculture and hydro-electric power.

Highland & Forests

Diverse Ecosystems

Cerro El Pital is the highest mountain and a cloud forest reserve with unique flora and fauna.
